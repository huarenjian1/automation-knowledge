往年考证明

$\delta(t-1)$

$f^{*}\:(\:t\:)\:=\:\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}f(nT)\:\delta(\:t\:-\:nT)$

$f^{*}(t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}f(nT)[u(t-nT)-u(t-nT-\tau)]$

将面积 $\tau f(nT)$ 作为强度 $f^{*}(t)=\tau f(nT)\delta_{T}(t)$

$$ \delta_T(\:t\:)\:=\:\sum_{n\:=\:-\infty}^{+\infty}\delta(\:t\:-nT)$$

$$ m_T(\:t\:)\:=\:\sum\limits_{k\:=\:-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\tau}\Big[\:u\:(\:t\:-\:kT)\:-\:u\:(\:t\:-\:kT\:-\:\tau)\:\Big]$$

$$ m_T(\:t\:)\:=\:\sum_{n=-\infty}^\infty C_n\:\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{j}n\omega_st}$$

$$ f^{*}\left(t\right)=\sum\limits_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}f(t)\delta(t-nT)=f(t)\sum\limits_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\delta(t-nT)=f(t)\delta_{T}(t)\quad(2.2.7)$$
